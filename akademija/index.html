Novosadska barokna akademija

Pedagoški centar festivalaThe festival’s pedagogical core

Akademija je namenjena studentima, mladim profesionalcima i nastavnicima zainteresovanim za ranu muziku, istorijski informisano izvođenje i rad sa specijalizovanim predavačima.The academy is intended for students, young professionals, and teachers interested in early music, historically informed performance, and work with specialist faculty.

Izdanje 2026 okuplja Meilu Tomé, Radoslavu Vorgić, Rafaela Arjonu Ruza, Bojanu Dimković i Héctora Hervása. Informacije o prijavama i satnici biće objavljene uskoro.The 2026 edition brings together Meila Tomé, Radoslava Vorgić, Rafael Arjona Ruz, Bojana Dimković, and Héctor Hervás. Application details and schedule information will be announced soon.
