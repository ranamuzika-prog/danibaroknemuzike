Program za decu

Rana muzika za novu publikuEarly music for new audiences

Festivalski program za decu razvija pažljivo osmišljene formate koji najmlađoj publici približavaju zvuk, instrumente i repertoar rane muzike.The festival’s programme for children uses carefully designed formats to introduce young audiences to the sounds, instruments, and repertoire of early music.

U okviru izdanja 2026. godine biće održan „Barok za najmlađe“ – interaktivni koncert upoznavanja sa ranom muzikom, namenjen deci uzrasta od 5 do 8 godina. Deca će moći da razgovaraju sa muzičarima, upoznaju istorijske instrumente, čuju kako oni zvuče, kao i da pevaju i plešu uz muziku ansambla Musica Antiqua Neoplantensis.As part of the 2026 festival, “Baroque for the Youngest” will be presented as an interactive discovery concert for children aged 5–8. Children will be able to talk to the musicians, discover historical instruments and hear how they sound, as well as sing and dance to music performed by Musica Antiqua Neoplantensis.

Program će biti održan 8. oktobra u 10:00, u Dečijem kulturnom centru Novi Sad, Radnička 20. Trajanje programa je oko 45 minuta, a učešće je besplatno.The programme will take place on 8 October at 10:00 at the Novi Sad Children’s Cultural Centre, Radnička 20. It will last approximately 45 minutes, and participation is free.
