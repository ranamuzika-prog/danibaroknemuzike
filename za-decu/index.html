Program za decu

Rana muzika za novu publikuEarly music for new audiences

Festivalski program za decu razvija pažljivo osmišljene formate koji približavaju istorijske instrumente, zvuk i repertoar najmlađoj publici.The festival’s children’s programme develops carefully designed formats that bring historical instruments, sound, and repertoire closer to young audiences.

U okviru izdanja 2026 posebno mesto ima radionica Barok za najmlađe za decu uzrasta 5–8 godina, 8. oktobra u Dečijem kulturnom centru Novi Sad, Radnička 20, u terminima 10:00 i 11:00. Radionica traje oko 45 minuta. Učešće je besplatno.Within the 2026 edition, Baroque for the Youngest is a free workshop for children aged 5–8, held on 8 October at the Children’s Cultural Centre of Novi Sad, Radnička 20, at 10:00 and 11:00. The workshop lasts about 45 minutes.
