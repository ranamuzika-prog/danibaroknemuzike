Program okuplja koncertnu operu, ansamblsku duhovnu muziku, iberijsku instrumentalnu tradiciju, radionicu za najmlađe i trodnevnu akademiju.

The programme brings together concert opera, ensemble sacred music, Iberian instrumental traditions, a children’s workshop, and a three-day academy.
