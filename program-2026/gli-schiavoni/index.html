Gostovanje iz HrvatskeGuest ensemble from Croatia

Gostovanje ansambla Gli Schiavoni donosi program Canticum Canticorum, usmeren ka susretu duhovnog, poetskog i zvučnog sveta ranog novog veka. U akustici crkve Sv. Jurja koncert dobija dodatnu prostornu i simboličku dimenziju.The visit of Gli Schiavoni brings the programme Canticum Canticorum, focused on the meeting point of sacred, poetic, and sonic worlds in the early modern era. In the acoustics of the Church of St George, the concert gains an added spatial and symbolic dimension.

Ovaj događaj se nadovezuje na festivalsku temu Raskršća kroz ideju prelaska tekstova, muzičkih obrazaca i izražajnih modela između različitih kulturnih sredina.This event aligns with the festival theme Crossroads through the movement of texts, musical patterns, and expressive models across different cultural settings.

Akustički okvirAcoustic setting

Crkveni prostor podržava kontemplativni karakter programa i jasnoću vokalnih linija.The church setting supports the contemplative character of the programme and the clarity of the vocal lines.

Tematska vezaThematic link

Program otvara prostor za slušanje muzike kao jezika duhovne i kulturne razmene.The programme opens a space for hearing music as a language of spiritual and cultural exchange.
