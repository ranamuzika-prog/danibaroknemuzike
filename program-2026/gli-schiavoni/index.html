Ansambl Gli Schiavoni gostuje sa programom Canticum Canticorum, koji otvara prostor za meditativno slušanje duhovnog repertoara i za nijansiranu ansamblsku interpretaciju.The ensemble Gli Schiavoni appears with Canticum Canticorum, a programme that opens a space for meditative listening to sacred repertoire and nuanced ensemble interpretation.

Smeštanje koncerta u crkvu Sv. Jurja dodatno naglašava akustičku i duhovnu dimenziju programa.Presenting the concert in the Church of St George further emphasises the programme’s acoustic and spiritual dimension.
