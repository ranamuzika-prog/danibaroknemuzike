Pedagoško jezgro festivalaThe pedagogical core of the festival

Novosadska barokna akademija povezuje umetnički i pedagoški segment festivala kroz rad sa studentima, mladim profesionalcima i nastavnicima zainteresovanim za ranu muziku i istorijski informisano izvođenje. Ona je prostor razmene znanja, prakse i iskustva unutar festivalskog okvira.The Novi Sad Baroque Academy connects the artistic and pedagogical strands of the festival through work with students, young professionals, and teachers interested in early music and historically informed performance. It is a space for the exchange of knowledge, practice, and experience within the festival framework.

Akademija obuhvata individualni i grupni rad, mentorski pristup i završni javni program, čime festivalsku temu prevodi u obrazovni proces.The academy includes individual and group work, a mentoring approach, and a final public presentation, translating the festival theme into an educational process.
