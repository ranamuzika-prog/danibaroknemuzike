O akademijiProgramPredavačiLokacija

Novosadska barokna akademija povezuje umetnički i pedagoški segment festivala kroz rad sa studentima, mladim profesionalcima i nastavnicima zainteresovanim za ranu muziku i istorijski informisano izvođenje.The Novi Sad Baroque Academy connects the festival’s artistic and pedagogical strands through work with students, young professionals, and teachers interested in early music and historically informed performance.

Akademija obuhvata individualni i grupni rad, razmenu iskustava i završni javni program.The academy includes individual and group work, exchange of experience, and a final public presentation.
