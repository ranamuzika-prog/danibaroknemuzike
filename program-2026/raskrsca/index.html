Raskršća 2026

Muzika iz vremena Servantesa

Tema izdanja 2026 istražuje ranu modernu Evropu kao prostor kretanja: umetnika, knjiga, instrumenata, tekstova i ideja. U toj mreži Iberijsko poluostrvo, Mediteran i srednjoevropski gradovi učestvuju u stalnoj razmeni stilova i praksi.

The 2026 theme explores early modern Europe as a space of movement: of artists, books, instruments, texts, and ideas. Within that network, the Iberian Peninsula, the Mediterranean, and Central European cities participate in a constant exchange of styles and practices.

Novi promotivni materijal za 2026 uskoro

Servantesovo vreme obeleženo je putovanjima, prevodima i susretima kultura. Muzičari prelaze granice između dvora, crkve i grada; repertoari se preoblikuju u novim sredinama; instrumenti nose lokalne boje i transnacionalne uticaje. Otuda naslov Raskršća: on označava mesta susreta, pregovora i preplitanja.Cervantes’s age was marked by travel, translation, and cultural encounters. Musicians crossed boundaries between court, church, and city; repertories were reshaped in new settings; instruments carried both local colour and transnational influence. Hence the title Crossroads: it names places of meeting, negotiation, and intertwining.

Program 2026 povezuje koncertnu operu G. F. Händela, iberijske žičane tradicije, duhovnu muziku ansambla Gli Schiavoni, radionički rad sa decom i pedagoški okvir Novosadske barokne akademije. Zajednički imenitelj nije samo stil već i ideja prenosa znanja kroz izvođenje, slušanje i obrazovanje.The 2026 programme connects G. F. Handel’s concert opera, Iberian plucked-string traditions, the sacred repertoire of Gli Schiavoni, workshop activity with children, and the pedagogical framework of the Novi Sad Baroque Academy. The common thread is not only style, but also the transfer of knowledge through performance, listening, and education.
