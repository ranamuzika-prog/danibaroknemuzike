Prvi susret sa ranom muzikomA first encounter with early music

Program Barok za najmlađe osmišljen je kao prvi susret sa ranom muzikom kroz slušanje, razgovor, demonstraciju istorijskih instrumenata i aktivno učešće dece. Cilj je da nova publika uđe u festivalski svet bez distance, kroz igru, radoznalost i neposredno iskustvo zvuka.Baroque for the Youngest is designed as a first encounter with early music through listening, conversation, demonstrations of historical instruments, and active participation by children. The goal is to welcome new audiences into the festival world through play, curiosity, and direct experience of sound.

Ovaj događaj proširuje festivalsku publiku i povezuje umetnički program sa edukacijom, porodicama i najmlađim posetiocima.This event broadens the festival audience and connects the artistic programme with education, families, and the youngest visitors.

Za kogaWho it is for

Za decu, porodice i sve koji prvi put upoznaju ranu muziku.For children, families, and anyone encountering early music for the first time.

Kako izgledaWhat it looks like

Slušanje, demonstracija, razgovor i interaktivni zadaci prilagođeni mlađoj publici.Listening, demonstration, conversation, and interactive activities adapted for younger audiences.
