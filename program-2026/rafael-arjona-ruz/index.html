Solistički recitalSolo recital

Solistički recital Rafaela Arjone Ruza uvodi publiku u zvučni svet Španije iz vremena Servantesa. Vihuela, barokna gitara i lauta ovde nisu samo instrumenti nego i nosači kulturnih veza, prelaza i repertoarskih putanja koje oblikuju temu izdanja Raskršća.Rafael Arjona Ruz’s solo recital introduces audiences to the sound world of Spain in the time of Cervantes. Here, the vihuela, baroque guitar, and lute are not only instruments but carriers of cultural links, transitions, and repertory routes that shape the Crossroads theme.

Koncert je direktno povezan sa međunarodnim partnerstvima festivala i sa pedagoškim segmentom 2026, jer je umetnik ujedno i jedan od predavača Novosadske barokne akademije.The concert is directly tied to the festival’s international partnerships and to the pedagogical strand of the 2026 edition, as the artist is also one of the faculty members of the Novi Sad Baroque Academy.
