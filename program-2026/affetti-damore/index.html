Program Affetti d’amore vraća fokus na izraz, retoriku i emotivne registre ranobarokne muzike. U izvođenju ansambla Musica Antiqua Neoplantensis koncert razvija lokalnu izvođačku liniju festivala i povezuje je sa širim evropskim stilskim idiomima.The programme Affetti d’amore returns the focus to expression, rhetoric, and the emotional registers of early Baroque music. Performed by Musica Antiqua Neoplantensis, the concert extends the local performance line of the festival while connecting it to broader European stylistic idioms.

Naslov naglašava afekt, osećaj i muzičku retoriku kao ključne pokretače barokne interpretacije, čineći koncert važnim mostom između edukativne i koncertne dimenzije festivala.The title emphasizes affection, feeling, and musical rhetoric as central drivers of Baroque interpretation, making the concert an important bridge between the educational and performance dimensions of the festival.
