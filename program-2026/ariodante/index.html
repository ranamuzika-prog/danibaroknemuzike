Koncert otvaranjaOpening concert

Otvaranje festivala donosi koncertnu verziju Handelove opere Ariodante, u izvođenju ansambla New Trinity Baroque i solista Beogradske barokne akademije. Projekat povezuje izvođačku praksu, mentorski rad i međunarodnu saradnju, što ga čini jednim od centralnih događaja izdanja 2026.The festival opens with a concert performance of Handel’s opera Ariodante, performed by New Trinity Baroque and soloists of the Belgrade Baroque Academy. The project connects performance practice, mentorship, and international collaboration, making it one of the core events of the 2026 edition.

U koncertnoj verziji fokus ostaje na muzičkoj retorici, vokalnoj virtuoznosti i dramskom luku partiture. Time se publici približava veliko scensko delo kroz precizno slušanje i izvođenje na istorijski osvešćen način.In concert form, the focus remains on musical rhetoric, vocal virtuosity, and the dramatic arc of the score. This allows audiences to encounter a major stage work through close listening and historically aware performance.

Zašto je važnoWhy it matters

Ariodante povezuje festivalsku umetničku liniju sa obrazovnom misijom, jer okuplja afirmisani ansambl i mlade pevače u zajedničkoj produkciji.Ariodante connects the festival’s artistic line with its educational mission by bringing together an established ensemble and young singers in a shared production.

Fokus slušanjaListening focus

Vokalni dijalozi, kontrasti afekata, orkestarske boje i ritam dramske napetosti.Vocal dialogue, contrasting affections, orchestral colour, and the pacing of dramatic tension.
