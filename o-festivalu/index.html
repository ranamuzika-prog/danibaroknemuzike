O festivalu

Festival rane muzike u Novom SaduAn early music festival in Novi Sad

Dani barokne muzike je festival rane muzike osnovan u Novom Sadu, posvećen renesansnom i baroknom repertoaru i istorijski informisanom izvođenju. Kroz koncerte, akademiju i posebne projekte festival stvara prostor susreta međunarodnih umetnika, regionalnih ansambala, studenata i lokalne publike.Days of Baroque Music is an early music festival founded in Novi Sad, dedicated to Renaissance and Baroque repertoire and historically informed performance. Through concerts, an academy, and special projects, it creates a meeting point for international artists, regional ensembles, students, and local audiences.

Jedan od glavnih ciljeva festivala je razvoj održive scene rane muzike u Vojvodini i jačanje veza Novog Sada sa evropskim kulturnim mrežama.One of the festival’s central aims is to build a sustainable early music scene in Vojvodina and strengthen Novi Sad’s links with European cultural networks.
