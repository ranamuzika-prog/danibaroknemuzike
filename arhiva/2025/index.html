Izdanje 2025

Deseto izdanje festivalaThe tenth festival edition

Deseto izdanje Dana barokne muzike 2025. godine donelo je sedam koncerata. Septembarski i oktobarski programi održani su u crkvi Sv. Elizabete u Telepu, zatim dva kamerna resitala u Muzeju Vojvodine i završnu operu u Novosadskoj sinagogi.The tenth edition of Days of Baroque Music in 2025 presented seven concerts. September and early October programmes took place in the Church of St Elizabeth in Telep, followed by two chamber recitals at the Museum of Vojvodina and a final opera production in the Novi Sad Synagogue.

Program

12.09.2025 — Iberian Ensemble (Portugal i Brazil), crkva Sv. Elizabete, Telep

19.09.2025 — Dom Muza & Visantiqua (Rusija i Srbija), crkva Sv. Elizabete, Telep

04.10.2025 — Mare Nostrum (Italija), crkva Sv. Elizabete, Telep

10.10.2025 — Musica Antiqua Neoplantensis (Srbija), crkva Sv. Elizabete, Telep

29.10.2025 — From Sorrow to Peace — Sam Brown i Ana Torbica, Muzej Vojvodine

03.11.2025 — Echoes — Bojan Čičić, Muzej Vojvodine

15.11.2025 — Opera Alcina — solisti Beogradske barokne akademije i New Trinity Baroque, Novosadska sinagoga
