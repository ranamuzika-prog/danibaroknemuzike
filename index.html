Days of Baroque Music – Novi Sad Dani barokne muzike – Novi Sad

Early music festival in the heart of Vojvodina Festival rane muzike u srcu Vojvodine

Days of Baroque Music is the first festival in Vojvodina dedicated to Historically Informed Performance of Early Music. Each autumn, we bring together musicians, students and audiences to rediscover Medieval, Renaissance and Baroque repertoire in Novi Sad. Dani barokne muzike je prvi festival u Vojvodini posvećen istorijski informisanom izvođenju rane muzike. Svake jeseni okupljamo umetnike, studente i publiku kako bismo zajedno ponovo otkrivali renesansni i barokni repertoar u Novom Sadu.

We are currently working on the programme for the next edition of the festival. Full details will be announced soon. Trenutno radimo na programu sledećeg izdanja festivala. Detaljan program biće objavljen uskoro.

Festival Festival

Days of Baroque Music (Dani barokne muzike) is an early music festival founded in Novi Sad, Serbia. The festival is dedicated to Early Music repertoire and to Historically Informed Performance practice. Dani barokne muzike je festival rane muzike osnovan u Novom Sadu. Festival je posvećen renesansnom i baroknom repertoaru i istorijski informisanom izvođenju.
Through concerts, a baroque academy and special projects, the festival creates a meeting point for international artists, regional ensembles, students and local audiences. Our aim is to build a sustainable Early Music scene in Vojvodina and to open Novi Sad towards European cultural networks. Kroz koncerte, baroknu akademiju i posebne projekte, festival postaje mesto susreta međunarodnih umetnika, regionalnih ansambala, studenata i lokalne publike. Naš cilj je da izgradimo održivu scenu rane muzike u Vojvodini i da otvorimo Novi Sad ka evropskim kulturnim mrežama.

Festival concerts Festivalski koncerti

Carefully curated programmes of Early Music music with period instruments and specialised ensembles. Pažljivo osmišljeni programi rane muzike na istorijskim instrumentima i sa specijalizovanim ansamblima.

Novi Sad Baroque Academy Novosadska barokna akademija

Masterclasses and workshops for students, young professionals and teachers interested in Early Music. Majstorski kursevi i radionice za studente, mlade profesionalce i profesore koji žele da se bave ranom muzikom.

Community projects Zajednički projekti

Performances and activities that bring early music closer to local communities and new audiences. Koncerti i aktivnosti kojima ranu muziku približavamo lokalnoj zajednici i novoj publici.

Edition 2025 Izdanje 2025. godine

The 10th edition of Days of Baroque Music in 2025 presented seven concerts. The September and early October programmes were held in the Church of St Elizabeth in Telep, followed by two chamber recitals at the Museum of Vojvodina and a final opera production in the Novi Sad Synagogue. Deseto izdanje Dana barokne muzike 2025. godine donelo je sedam koncerata. Septembarski i oktobarski programi održani su u crkvi Sv. Elizabete u Telepu, zatim dva kamerna resitala u Muzeju Vojvodine i završnu operu u Novosadskoj sinagogi.

  • 12.09.2025 – Iberian Ensemble (Portugal & Brazil), Church of St Elizabeth, Telep
  • 19.09.2025 – Dom Muza & Visantiqua (Russia & Serbia), Church of St Elizabeth, Telep
  • 04.10.2025 – Mare Nostrum (Italy), Church of St Elizabeth, Telep
  • 10.10.2025 – Musica Antiqua Neoplantensis (Serbia), Church of St Elizabeth, Telep
  • 29.10.2025 – From Sorrow to Peace – Sam Brown (lute, UK) & Ana Torbica (soprano), Museum of Vojvodina
  • 03.11.2025 – Echoes – Bojan Čičić (baroque violin, Croatia–UK), Museum of Vojvodina
  • 15.11.2025 – Opera Alcina (G. F. Handel) – Soloists of Belgrade Baroque Academy & New Trinity Baroque, conductor Predrag Gosta, Novi Sad Synagogue

Edition 2024 Izdanje 2024. godine

The 2024 edition of Days of Baroque Music focused on vocal and instrumental Baroque repertoire, with special attention to collaboration between local musicians and international guests. Izdanje 2024. godine bilo je posvećeno vokalnom i instrumentalnom baroknom repertoaru, sa posebnim naglaskom na saradnju lokalnih muzičara i međunarodnih gostiju.
